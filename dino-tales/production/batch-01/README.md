# DINO CONTOS — Production Batch 01

Five complete episodes. Start with the character sheet.

- [Character Reference Sheet & Style Guide](00-character-sheet.md)

## Episode concepts

| # | Title | Setting | Central conflict | Lesson | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [The Moonflower Seed](01-the-moonflower-seed.md) | The Whispering Meadow and Whispering Hill at night, under a sky full of shooting stars | A falling glowing seed is losing its light and must be planted on the hilltop before the moon goes down | The most wonderful things often need a little care and a lot of patience. | 6:54 |
| 2 | [Pipoca's First Snow](02-pipocas-first-snow.md) | A snowy pine valley on a bright winter morning, with frosty hills, a hollow log and Pedrão's cave | Pipoca is afraid of the strange cold snow, and later a tiny ankylosaurus is too scared to cross the snowy field to get home | New things can feel scary until we try them with a friend, and once we are brave, we can help someone else be brave too. | 7:02 |
| 3 | [The Secret Behind the Waterfall](03-the-secret-behind-the-waterfall.md) | A rainbow waterfall in the deep forest, a crystal cave hidden behind it and a secret underground garden | The crystal path inside a hidden cave goes dark, and a secret garden is wilting because its only sunlight window is blocked | A little creativity can bring light to even the darkest places, and the most beautiful treasures are the ones we take care of. | 6:38 |
| 4 | [Bico's Big Sky](04-bicos-big-sky.md) | Sunny sea cliffs, a sandy beach with dunes and a turquoise lagoon, ending in a golden sunset sky festival | Bico the young pteranodon is too afraid to fly high, and the Sky Festival is tonight | When friends believe in us, it becomes easier to believe in ourselves. | 6:45 |
| 5 | [A Gift for Mama Tetê](05-a-gift-for-mama-tete.md) | A colorful flower meadow and riverbank on a spring day, a sudden rain shower and a rainbow afterward | Pipoca's perfect surprise gift for its mother is ruined by a sudden rainstorm | The best gifts are not perfect things; they are made with love. | 6:40 |

Narration-only scripts for voice recording are in `narration/`.
