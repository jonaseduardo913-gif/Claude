# DINO CONTOS — Character Reference Sheet & Style Guide

This sheet is permanent. Every prompt in every episode repeats these exact descriptions. Never change colors, clothing, proportions or facial features.

## Global visual style

> High-end 3D cinematic children's animation, stylized animated-feature-film look, soft rounded shapes, smooth clay-like materials with subtle subsurface scattering, vibrant but gentle colors, soft global illumination, warm rim light, shallow depth of field, magical storybook atmosphere.

Every image prompt ends with: *16:9 aspect ratio, widescreen. No text, no subtitles, no captions, no logos, no watermarks, no extra characters, no humans other than Tuca, no realistic gore, nothing scary.*

Animation rule used in every video prompt:

> Keep every character on-model: same proportions, colors, clothing and facial features as in the source image. Natural, weighty, cartoon-appealing motion with gentle squash and stretch only on bounces; no morphing, no extra limbs, no face distortion, no flicker. 24 fps cinematic look, no text on screen.

## Main and recurring characters

### Tuca — Main character — curious little caveboy

- **Physical appearance:** Chubby, rounded body with an oversized head (head is about 40% of his height). Big rounded bare feet. Short stubby arms and legs.
- **Colors:** Skin warm tan (#E2A477). Hair dark brown (#3B2414). Tunic orange (#F0A23A) with dark-brown spots (#7A3F12). Cheeks pink (#FF9A8A).
- **Proportions:** About 3 heads tall. Reference height = 1.0 (all other sizes are given relative to Tuca).
- **Clothing / accessories:** One-shoulder orange tunic with leopard spots, strap over the right shoulder. No shoes, no jewelry, no hat.
- **Facial features:** Huge glossy brown eyes with white highlights, big round button nose, one big front tooth visible when smiling, expressive thick brows, rosy cheeks.
- **Personality:** Curious, brave in a small way, gentle, a little clumsy, always ready to help. Shows feelings with his whole body.
- **Size:** 1.0 (reference).

**Prompt description (copy exactly):**

```
Tuca, a cute 6-year-old cartoon caveboy with an oversized round head, big glossy chestnut-brown eyes, a large round button nose, rosy pink cheeks, one big white front tooth, short spiky dark-brown hair, warm tan skin, wearing a one-shoulder orange tunic with dark-brown leopard spots, barefoot with big rounded feet and short stubby rounded arms and legs
```

### Pipoca — Main character — Tuca's baby triceratops best friend

- **Physical appearance:** Round, chubby baby dinosaur with a big head, short chunky legs and a short tail.
- **Colors:** Skin lime green (#9AD46A). Belly cream-yellow (#FFF1B8). Frill orange (#FFB03A) with darker scallops (#F08A2A). Back spots pale green (#C8EF9C).
- **Proportions:** Body is a soft oval; head is half the body length. Tail is short.
- **Clothing / accessories:** Always wears half of the cream eggshell it hatched from as a little hat, tilted slightly to the right.
- **Facial features:** Huge glossy dark eyes, tiny cream horn nubs, small beak-like snout, pink cheek blush, wide happy mouth.
- **Personality:** Playful, affectionate, easily excited, a bit impatient, very brave when a friend needs help. Wags its tail when happy.
- **Size:** About 0.5 of Tuca's height (top of its frill reaches Tuca's waist).

**Prompt description (copy exactly):**

```
Pipoca, a tiny baby triceratops only as tall as Tuca's waist, soft lime-green skin, a pale cream-yellow belly, lighter green spots along the back, a small round orange frill with a darker orange scalloped edge, two tiny cream horn nubs, huge glossy dark eyes with white highlights, pink cheek blush, chubby short legs with cream toenails, a short tapered tail, wearing a half cream eggshell with a zigzag edge as a little hat
```

### Mama Tetê — Recurring — Pipoca's gentle mother

- **Physical appearance:** Massive, rounded body on four thick legs; long tapering tail.
- **Colors:** Skin #5AA86A, frill #E8703A, belly cream, bow pink #FF5D8F.
- **Proportions:** Large head with a wide frill; body very round.
- **Clothing / accessories:** Small pink bow on the top left of her frill.
- **Facial features:** Long eyelashes, big warm eyes, pink cheeks, gentle smile.
- **Personality:** Warm, protective, patient, a little dramatic when worried.
- **Size:** About 3.5x Tuca's height at the shoulder.

**Prompt description (copy exactly):**

```
Mama Tetê, a huge gentle mother triceratops, deep leaf-green skin, a cream belly, a large orange frill with a burnt-orange scalloped rim, two long cream brow horns and a short nose horn, long dark eyelashes, pink cheeks, kind sleepy-sweet eyes and a small pink bow on her frill
```

- **Clothing / accessories:** None.

```
Bico, a young cartoon pteranodon about as tall as Tuca when standing, lavender-purple body with a pale lilac belly, large soft lavender wing membranes, a long yellow-orange beak, a red-orange crest on the back of his head, huge round goofy eyes and thin purple legs with orange toes
```

### Pedrão — Recurring — big sleepy ankylosaurus

- **Physical appearance:** Wide, low, rounded body like a cozy armored couch.
- **Colors:** Skin #C99A5E, armor #7D5A3A, spikes cream #F1E4C4.
- **Proportions:** Long, low body; small head.
- **Clothing / accessories:** None.
- **Facial features:** Heavy sleepy eyelids, big round nose, tiny smile.
- **Personality:** Calm, slow, sleepy, kind, sneezes when tickled.
- **Size:** About 1.2x Tuca's height tall and 4x long.

**Prompt description (copy exactly):**

```
Pedrão, a big gentle cartoon ankylosaurus, sandy-tan skin with a cream belly, rows of rounded brown armor studs and short cream side spikes, small sleepy half-closed eyes, a big round brown nose, short sturdy legs and a tail ending in a round brown club
```
